# Product Brief

AstraView is an interactive, real-time orbit explorer that makes the world of satellites legible at a glance. It emphasizes immediate motion, intuitive navigation, and simple breakdowns so people can answer “what’s up there?” in seconds.

## Value Proposition
- Instant delight: open the site and see motion immediately.
- Clarity: browse orbit regimes and object types without technical jargon.
- Confidence: a trust panel explains definitions, freshness, and limitations.
- Shareability: one link recreates the exact view for collaboration.

## Audience
- Space-curious users looking for a quick answer.
- Educators and students needing visual context.
- Analysts and journalists seeking a fast overview.

## Differentiators
- Minimal chrome with a product-first globe experience.
- Fast filters with no apply buttons.
- Progressive detail with density cues at scale.
- Explicit trust guardrails to avoid misuse.
